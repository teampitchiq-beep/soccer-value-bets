Trends · Last 10 Matches
For every team playing on the chosen day: the most demanding line (Over / Under) that its last 10 matches, home and away together, still cleared. For = the team's own number, Against = what it conceded, Match total = both teams. Model is the probability of that line in this fixture (opponent-adjusted expected value with a negative binomial spread). A streak describes the past; the model says how likely it is now.

Team Shots · Last {{ shots_window }} Home / Away

Expected shots (xS) work like xG: the home team's shots-for strength against the away team's shots-conceded weakness (and the reverse), fitted on all shots history with recency weighting and opponent adjustment.
